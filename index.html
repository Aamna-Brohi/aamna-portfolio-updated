The Story

From a small city, to big ideas.

I grew up in Jacobabad, and my education has been carried by scholarships and the support of people who believed in it, from The Citizens Foundation to the Sindh Talent Hunt Program at Sukkur IBA.

That is why I build for education. I believe technology should reduce manual work, simplify processes and open doors for students who don’t have a guide, a counsellor or a second chance.

Today my work sits where engineering meets research. I build RAG systems, AI agents and full-stack products like UniPath AI and Ilmora Labs, and I lead a proposed ten-country study on how generative AI is reshaping university teaching. I look for real gaps, and I stay with them until the solution works for the people it was built for.

The Work

Projects that solve real problems.

UniPath AI

RAG-based university guidance for Pakistani Intermediate students

The Problem

After Intermediate, students struggle to find the right program, university, scholarship and test preparation, with little reliable guidance.

The Solution

Interest discovery, program and university matchmaking, application guidance and test prep, with answers grounded in official sources via RAG.

Development Status

Research, planning and initial development phase; built in collaboration with teammates Shivani and Tayaba.

Python FastAPI Next.js Tailwind CSS PostgreSQL ChromaDB / Qdrant LangChain / LlamaIndex

Ilmora Labs

B2B SaaS for MDCAT preparation academies

The Problem

Academies handle assessments, student records and performance tracking by hand, which is slow, error-prone and repetitive.

The Solution

One unified system for fees, tests and student records, with assessment intelligence and automated reporting so teachers can teach.

Long-Term Vision

Support the wider digital transformation and AI integration of educational academies across Pakistan.

Team of five: Aamna (CEO), Ramzan (Technical), Tayyaba (AI/ML and Data), Simran (Product and UI/UX), Tahir (Business Development). Dashboard shows sample data.

EdTech B2B SaaS Analytics Workflow Automation FastAPI PostgreSQL
